AI Voice Automation with n8n

Business Automation · API Integration

My strongest build. When a call ends, the voice agent sends the details by webhook to n8n. An AI agent there analyses the data and routes it — qualified leads to one Sheet, people who are delaying to a follow-up Sheet, and not-interested contacts dropped.

  • Built the full automation myself, practised on it a lot
  • Tried every voice on VAPI and ElevenLabs
  • Tested end to end with real test calls, many times

Honest status: the n8n workflow was on a free plan that expired, so the saved workflow was lost. I can rebuild it — the flow and the testing are mine.

Frequently asked questions

Can you build an AI agent for my business?

Yes — and here is how we build it. First, I understand your problem: what takes your time, what gets missed, and what frustrates your customers. Then I analyse your business: how your calls, messages and daily work actually flow. Then I solve that one problem first, with a small, simple agent — and automate the repetitive tasks around it. You test it with real calls and messages, and we improve it step by step. Tell me your business and your biggest daily headache, and I will tell you honestly what I can build.

Are the projects on this site from real clients?

No. They are personal prototypes and concept demos I built and tested myself. They are labelled as such, and no client, revenue or result is invented. When I get paying clients, I will replace them with verified case studies.

What tools do you work with?

VAPI, ElevenLabs, n8n and Google Sheets — with webhooks connecting them. I have basic knowledge of APIs and appointment booking, and I am still learning CRM integrations.

How do we start working together?

Email me with your business, what your calls should do, and how many calls you get. I will reply with what I can build, what I need from you, and the next small step.
